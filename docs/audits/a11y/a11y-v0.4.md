## Scripted keyboard and focus checks (pass)

- **Column headers**: Week day, Month week and Annual month headers keep short
  visible text and have an unabbreviated accessible name ("Sun, Nov 1" →
  "Sunday, November 1"). Total headers have no extra name.
- **Date navigation**: `nav` "Date range"; Previous/Next work by keyboard and change
  the table caption.
- **Go to date popover**: Enter on the range title opens it with focus in the date
  field and `aria-expanded="true"`; Escape closes it, returns focus to the title
  button and sets `aria-expanded="false"`.
- **Actions menu**: Enter opens on the first item; Up from the first wraps to the
  last; Escape returns focus to "Actions for {name}".
- **Rename confirm dialog**: focus starts on the dialog container; Tab cycles
  Cancel → Confirm → Cancel; Escape cancels, closes the rename form and returns
  focus to the row's Actions button.
- **Add tooltip (Budget setup)**: shows on hover; Escape hides it (WCAG 1.4.13)
  without closing Budget setup.
- **Responsive**: no sideways page scroll at 320, 400, 768 and 1280px; toolbar and
  popover stay inside the viewport at 320–1440px and 200% zoom (checked in the
  v0.4.12 work); every visible control in the Month table, popover and Actions menu
  is at least 44×44px at 375px. No duplicate ids with Budget setup open.
