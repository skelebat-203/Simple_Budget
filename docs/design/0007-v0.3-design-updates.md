# v0.3 design updates

Source: `docs/human_scratch_files/stuff.md` (human notes), refined with the
human on 2026-11-02. Roadmap: `0003-roadmap.md` v0.3. Builds on
`0004-table-design.md` and `0006-prototype.html`.

Per doc precedence this doc wins where it conflicts with a lower-numbered doc.
Each conflict is listed under **Supersedes** so it is never silent.

## 1. Calendar settings

Both settings live in the Budget setup overlay (§5).

### 1.1 Week start
- The user picks the day a week starts on. Any day. **Default: Sunday.**
- Applies to the Week table and to the week columns in the Month table.

### 1.2 Month boundaries
- Two options:
  - **Whole weeks** (**default**) — the month starts on the first day of the
    week containing the 1st and ends on the last day of the week containing the
    month's last day. Every week column is complete.
  - **Calendar month** — the 1st to the last day of the month.

## 2. Category structure

Income, Savings and Expenses can all hold subcategories.

Top level, in order:

| Row | Contents |
|-----|----------|
| Income `*` | Pay, Incidental Income, and any custom subcategories |
| Taxes | Its own row; takes amounts directly |
| Net Income `*` | Income − Taxes |
| Savings `*` | Savings items and any custom subcategories |
| Expenses `*` | Expense subcategories (unchanged) |

- Pay and Incidental Income become Income subcategories that take amounts
  directly (as Groceries does under Expenses).
- Colors: Income uses blue at every level, the same as Pay and Incidental Income
  today.
- **Supersedes** `0004` top level (Pay, Incidental Income, Taxes as separate
  rows) and its Net Income formula (Pay + Incidental Income − Taxes). The total
  is the same; the structure changes.

### 2.1 Subcategory estimates
- A subcategory with **no** items takes a manually entered estimate.
- A subcategory **with** items has a **calculated** estimate: the sum of its
  items' estimates. It cannot be edited directly.
- If a subcategory has a manual estimate and the user creates its first item,
  the app:
  1. creates a **Miscellaneous** item in that subcategory,
  2. moves the subcategory's estimate (and recurrence and autopay) to it, and
  3. shows an info banner, e.g. "Your Utilities estimate moved to
     Utilities › Miscellaneous. Utilities now totals its items."
- Recurrence and autopay belong to whatever row holds the manual estimate.

## 3. Recurrence and estimates

- Every item and every subcategory with a manual estimate gets
  **Recurrence** and **Autopay**.
- Recurrence options: Daily, Weekly, Every 2 weeks, Bi-monthly (twice a month),
  Monthly.
- Debt, Utilities and Subscriptions items also get **Quarterly** and **Annual**.
- The Week and Month tables scale each estimate from its recurrence to the
  range shown. Example: a $120 quarterly bill shows $40 in a month and about
  $9.23 in a week.
- **Supersedes** `0002` UI/UX rule limiting Recurrence to income, debt,
  utilities and subscriptions, and Autopay to debt, utilities and subscriptions.

## 4. Add budget item form

- Fields: **Item**, **Amount**, **Date**. Estimated spend and Recurrence are
  removed; they are set only in Budget setup.
- **Supersedes** `0004` "Other": "When adding a new item or category, ask for an
  estimated spend plus a recurrence dropdown."

### 4.1 Item list
- Before the user types, the list shows **only the items selected in Budget
  setup**.
- Once the user types, autocomplete searches the **full** list (preloaded and
  custom).

### 4.2 Typing a new item
When the typed text matches no existing item:

1. A **Category** dropdown appears. It lists categories and subcategories.
   Categories are headings only and cannot be picked; they show where each
   subcategory sits.
2. If the user types a **new subcategory** name in Category, it is saved and a
   **Where?** dropdown appears directly below it: Income, Savings, Expenses.
3. A **Use in future** checkbox (**unchecked** by default) sits to the left of
   Save. Checked selects the new item (and subcategory) for regular use.
4. **Save is always on the right** of the form.
5. The new item, or new subcategory and item, always appears in the budget
   tables, whether or not *Use in future* is checked.

## 5. Budget setup overlay

### 5.1 Layout, top to bottom
1. Supporting text: "If you’d like to skip ahead just select “Save” at the
   bottom of the screen. Income, Tax, Emergency fund, Charity, and a few other
   items have been preselected for you."
2. **Budget table** (one table; replaces the checkbox lists, see 5.2).
3. **Week start** and **Month boundaries** settings (§1).
4. **Default display range**: a radio group with Day, Week, Month.
5. **Preview table** (5.4).
6. Footer, sticky to the bottom of the overlay: **Cancel** on the left,
   **Save** on the right.

- **Supersedes** `0006-prototype.html` and the v0.2.5 build: the "Select the
  items you think you’ll use regularly" checkbox lists, the "Show item list"
  button, and Save keeping the overlay open and collapsing the list.

### 5.2 Budget table
Columns: **Select**, **Item name**, **Estimated spending**, **Recurrence**,
**Autopay**, plus reorder controls (5.3).

- Uses the budget table's category › subcategory › item structure and colors.
- **Select** (first column) appears on every subcategory and item row.
  - A subcategory's checkbox selects or clears all of its items. Its accessible
    name says so, e.g. "Select all Utilities items".
  - Selection controls only two things: the checkbox state and whether the row
    appears in the preview table. It never clears estimates, recurrence or
    autopay.
- Estimated spending, Recurrence and Autopay follow §2.1 and §3. A subcategory
  with items shows its calculated estimate as read-only text.
- Estimated spending inputs keep the 0006 naming: `aria-labelledby` the column
  header and `aria-describedby` the item name.
- Each category and subcategory ends with a **Create** row: an input and an
  **Add** button (disabled until text is entered; `aria-describedby` the input).
- Recurrence keeps the side-by-side "per {select}" layout.

### 5.3 Reordering
- Each item and subcategory row has **Move up** and **Move down** buttons.
- Items also have a **Move to** select listing the subcategories in the same
  category.
- Items can change subcategory within their category. Items and subcategories
  cannot move to another category.
- The order is saved and used by the Item list and every table.

### 5.4 Preview table
- A read-only budget table using the user's current selections and estimates in
  the chosen default range. Updates live as they edit.

### 5.5 Save and Cancel
- **Save** saves everything and closes the overlay.
- **Cancel** closes without saving.
- Focus returns to the Set estimates button on close.

## 6. Navigation

- A **tab list** at the top with two tabs: **Add budget item** and **Budget**.
  One panel shows at a time (ARIA tabs pattern, arrow-key navigation).
- Replaces the v0.2.5 layout, where both sections stack on one page.

## 7. Budget tables

### 7.1 Ranges
The Budget tab offers **Day, Week, Month, Annual**.

### 7.2 Day — actuals only
- Shows only that day's transactions. No Est. column.
- **Supersedes** `0004` Day layout (Row 1: Est. and Act.).

### 7.3 Week and Month
- Unchanged from `0004`, except estimates scale per §3 and boundaries follow §1.

### 7.4 Annual — actuals only
- One **Act.** column per month, then a **Total** column for the year.
- No estimates.
- **Supersedes** `0004` Year layout (Est. and Act. per month).
- Not offered as a default display range (5.1).
